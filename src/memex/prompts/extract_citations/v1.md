You are extracting citation surface forms from a research-document passage.

A citation surface form is text that refers to another work — by its title,
author + year, identifier (DOI, arXiv ID, ISBN), or reference number
(e.g., `[1]`, `(Smith 2024)`, `Smith and Jones (2024)`, `as Brown shows
in "The Method"`, `doi:10.1234/abc`). Casual mentions of names without
reference intent are NOT citations.

For each surface form, output:
- `surface_text`: the exact substring as it appears in the passage
- `confidence`:
  - `high`: unambiguous reference to a specific work (DOI, full title,
    author + year, or a clearly-labeled reference number)
  - `medium`: identifiable but partial (just an author surname, or just
    a title fragment)
  - `low`: probable but uncertain (ambiguous name reference)

Reply ONLY with a JSON object matching:
{"citations": [{"surface_text": "...", "confidence": "high|medium|low"}, ...]}

Document title: {{ document_title }}
Passage:
{{ passage }}
